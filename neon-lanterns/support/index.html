FAQ

How do I play? Hang exactly one lantern in every row, every column and every coloured district. No two lanterns may touch — not even diagonally. Tap a cell once to mark it dark (×), twice to hang a lantern, three times to clear it. Drag across empty cells to darken many at once.

Is every puzzle solvable without guessing? Yes. Every board has exactly one solution and was checked by a solver that only uses logical deductions.

What do the dimmed cells mean? With "Beams" on, each lantern dims the cells it rules out (its row, column, district and the 8 cells around it). They are only a visual aid; your own × marks stay yours. You can turn beams off in Settings.

Why is a lantern pulsing red? It clashes with another lantern (same row, column or district, or touching), or a district has no free cell left. Turn "Show conflicts" off in Settings if you prefer.

How do hints work? HINT names the next deduction (for example "Only one spot left in this row") and highlights the cells. "Show me" applies it. On iOS and in the Daily, hints are free and unlimited. On Android each level has 3 free hints; after that “▶ Watch ad” gives one more (up to 3 per level), Premium gives them without a video, and restarting the level resets the count. Hints only affect stars: no hints = 3 stars, 1–2 hints = 2 stars, 3 or more = 1 star.

How does the Daily puzzle work? Everyone gets the same 8×8 puzzle each day, based on your device's date. Clear it on consecutive days to grow your streak, and share your time as plain text.

How do I unlock themes? Collect stars: Harbor at 45, Aurora at 150, Ember at 300 and Prisma at 600 stars.

I'm colour-blind. Districts are separated by thick glowing borders, and "Colour patterns" (on by default) gives every district its own fill pattern.

The cells are too small. Turn on "Large board" in Settings.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline. On Android, ads and the Premium purchase need a connection; the puzzles never do. On Android, anonymous gameplay analytics are sent when a connection is available.

Are there ads or purchases? iOS: no ads and no in-app purchases. Android: ads between levels (never in the Daily or the first levels) and optional videos for an extra hint; the one-time Premium purchase removes the ads. Use Settings → Restore purchases on a new device.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress — including the puzzle you are in the middle of — is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app (or, on Android, clearing its storage) resets it.

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We (Thanh Binh Games) run no servers and collect no personal data ourselves; on Android, Google AdMob and Google Play collect some data as described there.

Gameplay analytics (Android): from version 1.0.2 the app sends anonymous gameplay events (levels started/finished, hints, ads, the Premium screen) to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see which parts are too hard and improve them. No name, email or advertising ID; it follows your choice in Settings → Privacy options. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
