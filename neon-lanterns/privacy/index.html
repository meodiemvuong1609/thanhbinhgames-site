Neon Lanterns – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Neon Lanterns (the "App"), published by Thanh Binh Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Apple iOS and Google Android.

Summary: Neon Lanterns has no accounts and we (Thanh Binh Games) run no servers that receive your data. The game itself works offline and keeps your progress on your device. The Android version shows ads from Google AdMob and offers an optional one-time purchase ("Premium") through Google Play; Google, not us, processes the data these services need. The iOS version has no ads, no purchases and no third-party SDKs.

1. Information we collect

we do not collect personal information on our own servers. No account, no contacts/photos/camera/microphone, no precise location. On Android, anonymous gameplay statistics go to Google Analytics for Firebase (section 9).

2. Advertising (Android only)

the Android app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Before any ad is requested, the App asks Google's consent form where the law requires it (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland); you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options. When ads may be shown, Google may collect and process: the advertising ID / device identifiers, approximate location derived from the IP address, app interactions with ads, and diagnostic information, for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads appear only between levels or when you choose to watch a video for an extra hint; ads are rated PG and the App is not directed at children under 13. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites · Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

3. Purchases (Android only)

"Premium" (no ads, hint rewards without videos) is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or billing details. The App asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Premium (purchase history for this app only) and stores a "Premium owned" flag on your device so it works offline.

4. Information stored only on your device

in UserDefaults on iOS and SharedPreferences on Android: highest unlocked level and stars; the marks, timer and hint count of the puzzle you are solving; hints earned by watching ads on that puzzle; Daily streak, streak freezes, best streak, last cleared Daily date and today's best time; colour theme; whether the tutorial and tips were shown; settings; on Android the number of levels won (to keep the first levels ad-free) and the Premium flag. This stays on the device (except in your own device backups); uninstalling removes it.

5. Sharing initiated by you

SHARE after a Daily puzzle opens the system share sheet with a two-line plain text (date, board size, time, hints, streak; no link, no image). Nothing is sent unless you pick an app.

6. Sharing of information

we do not sell or share personal information. On Android, the data described in sections 2, 3 and 9 goes directly from your device to Google under Google's terms.

7. Children's privacy

the App is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children; AdMob requests are marked as not child-directed with a PG content cap.

8. Your rights

under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, Russian 152-FZ and Vietnamese law: we hold no personal data about you. For advertising data, use Settings → Privacy options in the App, your device's ad settings, or contact Google. Contact us with any question.

9. Analytics (Android only)

the Android app (version 1.0.2 and later) uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase SDK by Google) to see, in aggregate, how the game is played so we can improve the levels. It records gameplay events we define (a level or Daily started or solved with its level number, stars and time; a hint shown and whether it was free, from a rewarded video or from Premium; a rewarded video offered or earned; an in-between ad shown; the Premium screen opened, a purchase started or completed) and events Google collects automatically (first open, app update, session start, engagement time), together with a random Firebase app instance ID for this installation (new after a reinstall or clearing the App's data), device model, OS and app version, language and an approximate location Google derives from the IP address. It does not collect the advertising ID, your name, email, contacts or any text, and there is no crash-reporting SDK. Analytics starts with every Google Consent Mode v2 signal "denied"; after Google's consent form (UMP) resolves the App passes your choice on (in the EEA, UK and Switzerland analytics storage only with your consent to storing information on your device and to measurement); change it in Settings → Privacy options. Google processes the data for us as a service provider, encrypted in transit; we only see aggregated reports. Event-level data is kept for 2 months (Google Analytics retention setting). More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

10. Changes

we update this policy before any version that changes data handling; the effective date above shows the latest version.
