FAQ

How do I play? Tap a column to launch its front light ship onto the orbit around the mandala. The ship glides around the ring and lights every outermost dark cell of its own colour. A ship with light left after one lap parks in the tray; tap it to launch it again. Light every cell to complete the mandala.

What does “ORBIT JAMMED” mean? A ship came back from its lap while every tray space was full. Launch ships in a better order, or use +1 SLOT on the fail screen to add a tray space (up to 2 per attempt).

How are stars awarded? 3 stars if the number of ships that returned to the tray is at most the level's par, 2 stars for up to par + 3, otherwise 1 star. Using SWAP or +1 SLOT caps the result at 2 stars; hints never cost stars.

How do I get more helpers? HINT: +1 the first time you get 3 stars on a level (max 9). SWAP: +1 the first time you clear each boss level (max 5). +1 SLOT: +1 for your first Daily Mandala clear of the day (max 5). Helpers cannot be bought.

How does the Daily Mandala work? There is a new mandala each day based on your device's date. Clear it on consecutive days to grow your streak.

How do I unlock themes? Collect stars: Sunset at 30, Aurora at 90, Candy at 180 and Solar at 300 stars.

I'm colour-blind. Every colour also has its own shape (circle, triangle, square, diamond, star). The shapes are on by default and can be toggled in Settings.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Are there ads or purchases? No ads and no in-app purchases.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app (or, on Android, clearing its storage) resets it.

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We collect no personal data.
