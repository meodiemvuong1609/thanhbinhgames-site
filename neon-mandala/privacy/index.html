Neon Mandala – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Neon Mandala (the "App"), published by Thanh Binh Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Apple iOS and Google Android. The ads and the Premium purchase described in sections 1–2 are in the Android App only.

Summary: Neon Mandala has no accounts and we do not collect, store on our own servers, share or sell personal data. The game works offline. On Android the App shows ads from Google AdMob (Google processes some device data to show them, see section 1) and offers an optional one-time "Premium" purchase through Google Play that removes the ads (section 2). On Android, Google Analytics for Firebase also counts anonymous gameplay events (see "Analytics (Android)"); there is no other analytics or tracking SDK.

1. Advertising (Android, version 2.1.0 and later)

The Android App shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and asks for consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). There are no banners and no ads when the App opens or during a level:

  • an occasional full-screen ad may appear after you tap NEXT on a won level (never in your first levels, never in the Daily, at most about once every 3 minutes);
  • optional rewarded videos: only when you tap "▶ Watch ad" to get one more HINT or extra dock space (at most 3 per level, never in the Daily).

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process on its own servers: your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address (used for an approximate location), interactions with ads (views, taps), and diagnostics about the SDK (for example crashes and performance). Google uses this data for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention; it is encrypted in transit. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. See "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested; ads are only requested when that choice allows it. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy options. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Ad requests are marked as not child-directed and limited to content rated PG. The game itself, including the Daily, works fully offline; ads simply do not load without an internet connection.

2. Premium purchase (Android)

Premium is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. It removes the in-between ads and gives the rewarded helpers without the video; it does not unlock levels or change the rules. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The App only receives from Google Play whether Premium is owned or pending (and a purchase token used to confirm the purchase to Google Play), and remembers "Premium owned" on your device so it works offline. "Restore purchases" in Settings asks Google Play again.

3. Information we collect ourselves

We do not collect any information from you on servers of ours. Specifically, the App:

  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or other personal files;
  • uses no analytics SDK except Google Analytics for Firebase on Android (anonymous gameplay events, see "Analytics (Android)") and has no crash-reporting or tracking code of our own;
  • uses the internet only for those Google services (on Android it requests the network permissions for them); the game itself plays offline.

4. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few game settings locally on your device using the operating system's standard storage (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android):

  • highest unlocked level and the stars earned on each level;
  • the number of helpers you hold (HINT and extra dock spaces) and which album rewards were already given;
  • Daily picture streak, best streak, streak freezes, the date of the last Daily picture you cleared and the list of cleared daily dates (used to show them in your albums);
  • selected colour theme and which tips have been shown;
  • sound, haptics and colour-shape on/off settings;
  • Android: the number of levels won used to space out ads (adsWins) and whether Premium is owned (premium).

The Daily picture is chosen from your device's local date; the date is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere. The albums re-create your finished pictures from the level number on your device; no pictures are stored or uploaded.

This information is not sent to us. It may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) according to your device settings, which are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App (or, on Android, by clearing the App's storage).

5. Sharing initiated by you

When you tap SHARE in an album or on the results screen, the App creates an image of your picture on your device and opens your device's standard share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app or contact in that sheet; whatever you then share is handled by the app you choose, not by us.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information and do not share information with third parties ourselves. On Android, Google receives the data described in section 1 directly from its SDK to show ads, and Google Play processes purchases (section 2).

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. Ad requests are marked as not child-directed and limited to PG content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We do not transmit or store personal data on servers of ours. Game progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's operating system. Google's SDKs send their data over encrypted connections.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's personal data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. You can change your ad consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options and reset your advertising ID in Android settings. For data processed by Google, see Google's Privacy Policy. We hold no personal data about you on servers of ours; the locally stored game progress is fully under your control as described in section 4.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that version is released.

Analytics (Android, version 2.1.2 and later)

The Android App uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase SDK by Google) to see, in aggregate, how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard) so we can improve it. It is not used to build advertising profiles, and the App has no crash-reporting SDK (no Crashlytics).

  • Events: gameplay events we define (for example a level started, completed or failed with its level number, mode, stars, moves or time; a hint or helper used and whether it was free, from a rewarded video or from Premium; a rewarded video offered or earned; an in-between ad shown; the Premium screen opened, a purchase started or completed) and events Google collects automatically (first open, app update, session start, engagement time). Events never contain your name, email, contacts, account or any text you type.
  • Identifiers and device information: a random Firebase app instance ID created for this installation (a new one is created if you reinstall the App or clear its data), device model, operating system and app version, language, screen size. Google derives an approximate location (country, region, city) from the IP address. Analytics does not collect the advertising ID (we switched that collection off; the AdMob SDK described above handles its own data).
  • Consent: Analytics starts with every Google Consent Mode v2 signal set to "denied". When Google's consent form (UMP) has resolved, the App passes your choice on: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland analytics storage is granted only if you agreed to storing information on your device and to measurement, and the ad-related signals follow your ad choices; elsewhere it is granted. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Who receives it, for how long: the data is sent encrypted to Google, which processes it for us as a service provider under the Google Analytics terms; we only see aggregated reports in the Firebase console and do not sell it. Event-level data is kept for 2 months (the Google Analytics data-retention setting) and then deleted; aggregated reports without identifiers may be kept longer. More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.
