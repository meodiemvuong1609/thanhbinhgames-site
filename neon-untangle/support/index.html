Neon Untangle – Support

Neon Untangle is a puzzle game by Thanh Binh Games: drag glowing nodes until no two neon wires cross.

Questions

How do I move a node? Drag it and drop it on any dot of the grid. Dropping it on another node swaps the two. You can also tap a node and then tap a spot (this also works with TalkBack).

What counts as a crossing? Two wires cross when they meet anywhere except at a node they share. A node lying on a wire it does not belong to also counts, and so do two wires from the same node that lie on top of each other. Crossing wires are red and dashed, with a ring on each crossing.

Is every level solvable? Yes. Every level is built from an untangled layout and then scrambled, and the game's test suite checks all 200 levels and the Daily boards.

What does HINT do? It moves one node to where it sits in the untangled layout. A level solved with a hint gives at most 2 stars. You start with 3 hints and earn more with 3-star levels, boss levels and the Daily. The Daily has no hints.

Why didn't my move count? Dropping a node outside the grid puts it back, and a short message explains it. Dropping it back on its own spot is not a move.

Ads and Premium: the free game may show a full-screen ad after you tap NEXT on a win (never during play, never in the Daily). A video plays only when you tap "▶ Watch ad" for an extra hint. "Premium — no ads, forever" is a one-time purchase in Settings; use Restore purchases on a new device. Ad consent can be changed in Settings → Privacy options.

Where is my progress? Only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We run no servers and collect no personal data ourselves; ads and purchases are handled by Google.
