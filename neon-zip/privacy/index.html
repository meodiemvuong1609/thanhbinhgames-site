Neon Zip – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 (Android version 1.3.2)

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Neon Zip (the "App"), published by Thanh Binh Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Apple iOS/iPadOS and Google Android. From version 1.3.0 the Android App (Google Play and other Android stores) shows ads through Google AdMob, and on Google Play it offers an optional one-time "Premium" purchase; section 8 describes the data processing this involves. The iOS App contains no ads and no purchases. The web (browser) versions on game portals are described in section 9.

Summary: We (Thanh Binh Games) do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data, and we run no server for the App. The game itself works completely offline. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects certain data and shares it with Google to show ads (section 8), and Google Play handles the optional Premium purchase (section 8).

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information from you. Specifically, the App:

  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or other device data;
  • uses no analytics SDK except Google Analytics for Firebase on Android (anonymous gameplay events, see "Analytics (Android)") and has no crash-reporting or tracking code of our own;
  • needs no internet connection to play. On Android, the internet and network-state permissions are used only by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing (section 8); the iOS App does not connect to the internet.

2. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few game settings locally on your device using the operating system's standard storage (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences/DataStore on Android):

  • highest unlocked level and the stars earned on each level, kept separately for each difficulty (MEDIUM, HARD, BRUTAL) on Android, and the selected difficulty;
  • number of hints available;
  • Daily Puzzle and Daily BRUTAL streaks, best streaks and the date of the last Daily you cleared, and the streak freezes you hold (Android);
  • selected colour theme, which intro cards and whether the tutorial have been shown;
  • sound and haptics on/off settings;
  • Android: whether the optional Daily reminder is on and its time, your total number of wins, the app version for which the rating prompt was last shown, and whether Premium is unlocked (a cached yes/no, see section 8).

The Daily Puzzle is chosen from your device's local date; the date is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Optional Daily reminder (Android). The reminder is off by default. If you switch it on in the menu, the App asks for the system notification permission (Android 13 and later) and schedules a local notification on your device, up to 7 days ahead, at the time you choose. The App also uses the "receive boot completed" permission only to schedule it again after your device restarts, the App is updated or the time zone changes. The notification is created on your device; nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the App or in your device's notification settings.

Google Play rating card (Android, Google Play version). After you have won a few levels, the App may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating card, at most once per app version; Google decides whether it appears. The card is Google's own service and is governed by Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The App itself sends no data and receives nothing from it.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) according to your device settings, which are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App (or, on Android, by clearing the App's storage).

3. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not share or sell information with any third party. In the Android App, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects certain data and shares it with Google, as described in section 8. In the web versions, the game portal on which you play runs its own ads and data collection under its own privacy policy, as described in section 9.

4. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We collect no personal information at all; the Android App is intended for users aged 13 and over, is not directed at children and limits ad content as described in section 8. Purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval (Google Play parental controls / Family Link). Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

5. Security

No personal data is transmitted to or stored by us. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google; purchases use Google Play's secure billing system. Game progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's operating system.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's personal data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; the locally stored game progress is fully under your control as described in section 2.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled (for example by adding online features), we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that version is released.

8. Ads and Premium in the Android App

From version 1.3.0 the Android App (Google Play, Samsung Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps) shows ads via Google AdMob, a service provided by Google, and includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. The iOS App contains no ads and no third-party SDKs, and everything in sections 1–7 applies to it unchanged.

When ads appear. A full-screen ad may appear only after you tap NEXT on a win screen: never during a level, never in the Daily Puzzle, never in your first levels, and at most about once every three minutes. When you have no hints left, an optional "▶ WATCH AD" button lets you watch a rewarded ad for one more hint (up to 3 per level, never in the Daily Puzzle). There are no banner ads and no ads when the App opens.

What data is processed. We (Thanh Binh Games) still do not receive, store, share or sell any personal data, and we have no servers. However, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects the following data and shares it with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate the approximate location of your device);
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad/video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

According to Google, this data is encrypted in transit. To load ads, these versions request the internet and network-state permissions and the advertising ID permission.

Consent. Each time the App starts, it uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether a consent or privacy message is required in your region (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland under the GDPR, and in US states with applicable privacy laws). Ads are only requested after this check allows it, and personalised ads are only shown where you have consented and where permitted by law. Where required, a "PRIVACY OPTIONS" button on the main menu lets you review or change your choices at any time.

Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). Uninstalling the App stops all data processing by the App and the ads SDK.

How Google uses data. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Requests about data held by Google should be made to Google using the tools described there.

Ads and children. The Android App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. Ads are never shown during gameplay, only after a win or when you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad, and ad content is limited to material suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).

Premium (Google Play). "Premium" is an optional one-time in-app purchase sold through Google Play Billing. It removes the full-screen ads for good and gives the rewarded hints at once without a video; it does not unlock levels or change the rules. The purchase, your payment method and your purchase record are handled by Google in your Google account under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service; we never see your payment details, name or email address. The App asks Google Play whether your account owns Premium (when the App starts or returns to the screen, and when you tap "RESTORE PURCHASES") and stores a single yes/no value on your device. After a refund, Premium turns off the next time Google Play reports that your account no longer owns it. Android versions from other stores do not sell Premium.

Game progress. As described in section 2, your unlocked levels, stars, hints, Daily Puzzle streak, settings and the cached Premium yes/no value stay only on your device and are never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else.

9. Web versions played in a browser (game portals)

Neon Zip is also published as a web game that you play in a browser on game websites and platforms ("portals"), such as CrazyGames, Poki, GameDistribution, GameMonetize, Y8, Facebook Instant Games and itch.io. The web version itself does not collect, send or sell any personal data, and we (Thanh Binh Games) do not receive any data from it. It does not ask for an account, does not set cookies, contains no analytics or tracking code of our own, and does not send your progress to any server.

Information stored in your browser. To remember your progress, the web version saves the same game progress and settings as listed in section 2 (highest unlocked level, stars per level, hints, Daily Puzzle streak, best streak and last cleared date, colour theme, whether the tutorial has been shown, and sound/haptics settings) in your browser's local storage (localStorage) for the website that serves the game, under keys starting with neonzip.: highestUnlocked, levelStars, hints, theme, dailyStreak, bestDailyStreak, dailyLastCleared, tutorialSeen, soundOn, hapticsOn. This information stays in your browser; the game does not send it to us or to anyone else. You can delete it by clearing the site data for that website in your browser settings. If your browser blocks local storage (for example in some private-browsing modes), progress is kept only in memory and is lost when you close the game.

Ads and data collection by the portal. On most portals the game is shown inside the portal's website or app, and the portal loads its own software development kit (SDK) into the game page from its own servers. The game uses this SDK only to tell the portal when loading has finished, when gameplay starts and stops, and when an ad may be shown; it does not pass your game progress or any personal data to it. The portal (not us) operates the ads and decides which ads are shown, and the portal and its advertising partners may use cookies, device or advertising identifiers, your IP address and similar technologies, and may ask for your consent where required by law. This processing is the responsibility of the portal under its own privacy policy, and any consent or privacy choices are offered and managed by the portal. The itch.io version contains no ads SDK and shows no ads.

Facebook Instant Games. When you play on Facebook or Messenger, Meta provides the platform and the ads. The game does not request your Facebook name, profile picture, friends list or player ID; Meta processes data about your use of its services under Meta's Privacy Policy.

If you play the game on another website, that website's privacy policy applies to any data it collects.

Ads and children. Interstitial ads are never shown while a level is being played: they may appear before your first game (on portals that require it) or between levels, at most about once every three minutes, and rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. Age requirements, ad content and children's privacy protections on each portal are set by that portal.

Analytics (Android, version 1.3.2 and later)

The Android App uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase SDK by Google) to see, in aggregate, how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard) so we can improve it. It is not used to build advertising profiles, and the App has no crash-reporting SDK (no Crashlytics).

  • Events: gameplay events we define (for example a level started, completed or failed with its level number, mode, stars, moves or time; a hint or helper used and whether it was free, from a rewarded video or from Premium; a rewarded video offered or earned; an in-between ad shown; the Premium screen opened, a purchase started or completed) and events Google collects automatically (first open, app update, session start, engagement time). Events never contain your name, email, contacts, account or any text you type.
  • Identifiers and device information: a random Firebase app instance ID created for this installation (a new one is created if you reinstall the App or clear its data), device model, operating system and app version, language, screen size. Google derives an approximate location (country, region, city) from the IP address. Analytics does not collect the advertising ID (we switched that collection off; the AdMob SDK described above handles its own data).
  • Consent: Analytics starts with every Google Consent Mode v2 signal set to "denied". When Google's consent form (UMP) has resolved, the App passes your choice on: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland analytics storage is granted only if you agreed to storing information on your device and to measurement, and the ad-related signals follow your ad choices; elsewhere it is granted. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Who receives it, for how long: the data is sent encrypted to Google, which processes it for us as a service provider under the Google Analytics terms; we only see aggregated reports in the Firebase console and do not sell it. Event-level data is kept for 2 months (the Google Analytics data-retention setting) and then deleted; aggregated reports without identifiers may be kept longer. More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.
