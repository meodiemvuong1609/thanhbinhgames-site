FAQ

How do I play? Tap an arrow to send it flying off the board in the direction it points. It only flies if its path is clear; tapping a blocked arrow costs one of your 3 hearts. Clear every arrow to win the level.

How are stars awarded? 3 stars for no mistakes, 2 stars for one mistake, 1 star for two mistakes.

I'm stuck on a level. Every board can always be solved. Tap HINT to highlight a safe arrow. You earn an extra hint (up to 9) the first time you get 3 stars on a level. On Android, when your hints run out on a normal level, HINT shows “▶ WATCH AD”: a short video you choose to watch gives +1 hint (up to 3 per level, never in the Daily Puzzle); Premium gives it without a video.

How does the Daily Puzzle work? There is a new board each day based on your device's date. Clear it on consecutive days to grow your streak.

How do I unlock themes? Collect stars: Synth at 30, Ice at 90, Lava at 180 and Gold at 300 stars.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline. On Android only the ads and the Premium purchase use the internet. On Android, anonymous gameplay analytics are sent when a connection is available.

Are there ads or purchases? iOS: no ads and no in-app purchases. Android (from 1.3.0): the free game may show a full-screen ad after you tap NEXT on a win (not in your first 6 wins, at most every 3 minutes and every 3 levels, never during a level, never in the Daily Puzzle; no banners); a video plays only when you tap “▶ WATCH AD”. On Google Play, “Premium — no ads, forever” is an optional one-time purchase that removes those ads and gives the extra hints without a video; it unlocks no levels. Use the Premium sheet’s Restore purchases on a new device; ad consent can be changed with Privacy options in the menu (when shown). The browser versions on game portals show the portal’s ads (see the Privacy Policy).

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app (or, on Android, clearing its storage) resets it.

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We (Thanh Binh Games) run no servers and collect no personal data ourselves; on Android, Google AdMob and Google Play collect some data as described there.

Gameplay analytics (Android): from version 1.3.2 the app sends anonymous gameplay events (levels started/finished, hints, ads, the Premium screen) to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see which parts are too hard and improve them. No name, email or advertising ID; it follows your choice in Settings → Privacy options. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
