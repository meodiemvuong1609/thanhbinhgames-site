Neon Sand Sort – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 (Android version 1.1.2)

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Neon Sand Sort (the "App"), published by Thanh Binh Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Google Android (package com.thanhbinhgames.neonsandsort) and to any later version for Apple iOS.

Summary: We (Thanh Binh Games) run no servers and do not receive, store, share or sell your personal data. The game itself works completely offline and has no accounts. From version 1.1.0 the App shows ads served by Google AdMob and offers one optional in-app purchase, "Premium", sold through Google Play. When your device is online, Google AdMob and Google Play collect some data as described in sections 4 and 5; this data goes to Google, not to us. Premium removes the ads.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information from you ourselves. Specifically, the App:

  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or other personal files;
  • uses no analytics SDK except Google Analytics for Firebase on Android (anonymous gameplay events, see "Analytics (Android)") and has no crash-reporting or tracking code of our own;
  • uses the internet only for the Google AdMob ads and Google Play purchases described below; the game plays fully offline.

2. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few game settings locally on your device using the operating system's standard storage (SharedPreferences on Android):

  • highest unlocked level and the stars earned on each level;
  • the number of UNDO and +1 TUBE helpers you have earned and which boss rewards were already given;
  • Daily streak, best streak, the date you last played the Daily, your best number of moves of the day and the streak freezes you hold (0 to 2);
  • which tutorial tips have been shown;
  • whether you own Premium (a cached copy of Google Play's answer, so Premium also works offline) and how many levels you have won, which the App uses to space out ads;
  • sound, vibration and colour-symbol on/off settings;
  • whether the optional Daily reminder is on and its time, your total number of wins and the app version for which the rating card was last requested.

The Daily puzzle is chosen from your device's local date; the date is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Optional Daily reminder (Android). The reminder is off by default. If you switch it on in Settings, the App asks for the system notification permission (Android 13 and later) and schedules a local notification on your device, up to 7 days ahead, at the time you choose. The App also uses the "receive boot completed" permission only to schedule it again after your device restarts, the App is updated or the time or time zone changes. The notification is created on your device; nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the App or in your device's notification settings.

Google Play rating card (Android). After you have won a few times, the App may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating card, at most once per app version; Google decides whether it appears. The card is Google's own service and is governed by Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The App itself sends no data to the card and does not receive your rating or comment; if you choose to submit one, Google processes it under its own policy.

This game data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your device's own backups (for example Google backup or a device-to-device transfer) according to your device settings, which are governed by Google's privacy policy. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage.

3. Sharing initiated by you

When you tap SHARE on a level or Daily result, the App prepares a short text on your device (for the Daily: date, stars, number of moves, par, streak and coloured square emoji; for a level: the level number, stars, moves and par) and opens your device's standard share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app or contact in that sheet; whatever you then share is handled by the app you choose, not by us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd):

  • Where ads appear: a full-screen ad may appear only after you tap NEXT on a level's win screen — never at launch, never during play, never in the Daily mode, not before you have won 6 levels, and at most once every 3 minutes and every 3 levels. A rewarded video plays only when you tap a "▶ Watch ad" button to get an extra helper. There are no banner ads.
  • Data collected by the AdMob SDK: device and advertising identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), interactions with ads, diagnostic and performance information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google uses it to serve, measure and limit ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. It is sent to Google encrypted in transit and is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy options. Ads are only requested after the form allows it.
  • Your controls: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads). Buying Premium stops all ads.
  • Ads are limited to content rated PG and the App does not mark its traffic as directed to children.

5. In-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

"Premium — no ads, forever" is a one-time purchase sold by Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. To unlock Premium, the App asks Google Play whether your Google account owns Premium and keeps only that yes/no answer on your device. Google keeps your purchase history under its own policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Use Restore purchases in Settings to check again, for example on a new device.

6. Sharing of information

We do not share or sell any information. The data that AdMob and Google Play collect (sections 4 and 5) is collected by Google under its own policies.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads in the App are limited to content rated PG. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We transmit and store no personal data. Game progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's operating system. AdMob and Google Play send their data encrypted in transit.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's personal data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. We hold no personal data about you; your locally stored game progress is fully under your control as described in section 2. For data collected by Google, use Settings → Privacy options in the App and Google's own tools (https://myadcenter.google.com, https://myaccount.google.com).

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that version is released.

Analytics (Android, version 1.1.2 and later)

The Android App uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase SDK by Google) to see, in aggregate, how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard) so we can improve it. It is not used to build advertising profiles, and the App has no crash-reporting SDK (no Crashlytics).

  • Events: gameplay events we define (for example a level started, completed or failed with its level number, mode, stars, moves or time; a hint or helper used and whether it was free, from a rewarded video or from Premium; a rewarded video offered or earned; an in-between ad shown; the Premium screen opened, a purchase started or completed) and events Google collects automatically (first open, app update, session start, engagement time). Events never contain your name, email, contacts, account or any text you type.
  • Identifiers and device information: a random Firebase app instance ID created for this installation (a new one is created if you reinstall the App or clear its data), device model, operating system and app version, language, screen size. Google derives an approximate location (country, region, city) from the IP address. Analytics does not collect the advertising ID (we switched that collection off; the AdMob SDK described above handles its own data).
  • Consent: Analytics starts with every Google Consent Mode v2 signal set to "denied". When Google's consent form (UMP) has resolved, the App passes your choice on: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland analytics storage is granted only if you agreed to storing information on your device and to measurement, and the ad-related signals follow your ad choices; elsewhere it is granted. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Who receives it, for how long: the data is sent encrypted to Google, which processes it for us as a service provider under the Google Analytics terms; we only see aggregated reports in the Firebase console and do not sell it. Event-level data is kept for 2 months (the Google Analytics data-retention setting) and then deleted; aggregated reports without identifiers may be kept longer. More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.
