FAQ

How do I play? Tap a tube to lift it, then tap another tube. The top layers of one colour pour across, as many as fit. You can pour onto the same colour or into an empty tube. A tube filled with one colour glows and locks. Sort every colour to clear the level.

Why can't I pour there? When a pour is not allowed, a short line appears where you tapped and tells you why (for example: colours don't match, the tube is full, or the tube is already finished).

What is the white prism sand? Prism sand (from level 11) fits any colour: it rides along with the colour run it sits on, any colour may be poured onto pure prism sand, and it counts as any colour in a finished tube.

What is the old sand marked “?” Old sand (from level 21) is dim and its colour is unknown until it becomes the top layer. The chips under the move counter show how many hidden layers of each colour remain. Every level is checked so it can be solved without guessing.

What is a narrow neck? From level 41 some tubes have a narrow neck: they let only one layer through per pour, in or out.

How are stars awarded? 3 stars when your number of moves is at most the level's par, 2 stars when it is at most par plus a small margin (at least 2), otherwise 1 star. Using a HINT or +1 TUBE caps the level at 2 stars. UNDO does not give moves back.

How do I get more helpers? HINT is free and unlimited. UNDO: +1 the first time you get 3 stars on a level and +1 for your first Daily of the day. +1 TUBE: +1 the first time you clear each boss level (every 10th level). Each holds at most 9. When UNDO or +1 TUBE is used up on a normal level, the button shows “▶ Watch ad”: a short video you choose to watch gives +1 (up to 3 per level, never in the Daily). Premium gives that +1 without a video.

How does the Daily work? Every day everyone gets the same board, based on your device's local date. Play as often as you like; your best number of moves of the day is kept. UNDO and +1 TUBE are off in the Daily; HINT still works. Playing on consecutive days grows your streak, and every 7-day streak earns a streak freeze (❄, hold up to 2) that covers a missed day.

How does the Daily reminder work? It is off by default. Turn it on in Settings, allow notifications when Android asks (Android 13 and later) and pick a time. It is a local notification scheduled on your phone; it is not sent on a day you already finished the Daily.

I’m colour-blind. Every layer also shows a symbol for its colour (circle, triangle, square, diamond, star…). Symbols are on by default and can be switched off in Settings.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline. Only the ads and the Premium purchase use the internet. On Android, anonymous gameplay analytics are sent when a connection is available.

Are there ads or purchases? Android: the free game may show a full-screen ad after you tap NEXT on a win (not in your first 6 wins, at most every 3 minutes and every 3 levels, never during a level, never in the Daily; no banners). A video plays only when you tap “▶ Watch ad”. “Premium — no ads, forever” is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play: it removes those ads and gives the +1 rewards without a video; it unlocks no levels. On a new device use Settings → Restore purchases; ad consent can be changed in Settings → Privacy options (when shown).

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage resets it. A level in progress is not saved: closing the app restarts that level.

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We (Thanh Binh Games) run no servers and collect no personal data ourselves; Google AdMob and Google Play collect some data as described there.

Gameplay analytics (Android): from version 1.1.2 the app sends anonymous gameplay events (levels started/finished, hints, ads, the Premium screen) to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see which parts are too hard and improve them. No name, email or advertising ID; it follows your choice in Settings → Privacy options. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
