Neon Drop Wars – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Neon Drop Wars (the "App"), published by Thanh Binh Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Apple iOS/iPadOS and Google Android. From version 1.2.0, the Android App (from Google Play and from other Android stores) shows ads through Google AdMob; section 8 describes the data processing by the ads SDK. The Google Play version also offers an optional one-time purchase, "Premium" (section 3). The web (browser) versions on game portals are described in section 9.

Summary: We (Thanh Binh Games) do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data, and we have no servers. The game itself works offline. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the App collects some device and usage data and shares it with Google to show ads (section 8); you can review your ad privacy choices in the App's Settings. Buying "Premium" removes the ads shown between rounds. The iOS version contains no ads, no purchases and no third-party SDKs.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information from you. Specifically, the App:

  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or other device data;
  • uses no analytics SDK except Google Analytics for Firebase on Android (anonymous gameplay events, see "Analytics (Android)") and has no crash-reporting or tracking code of our own;
  • does not send your game progress anywhere.

On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google Play Billing library included in the App connect to Google; what they process is described in sections 3 and 8. The iOS version does not connect to the internet and contains no third-party SDKs.

2. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few game settings locally on your device using the operating system's standard storage (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android):

  • current level, best score and total wins;
  • selected team colour;
  • sound and haptics on/off settings, and whether the training round was played;
  • on Android from Google Play: a single "Premium owned" yes/no value, so ads stay off while you are offline.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) according to your device settings, which are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App (or, on Android, by clearing the App's storage).

3. In-app purchase "Premium" (Google Play)

The game is free. The optional one-time purchase "Premium — no ads, forever" removes the ads shown between rounds and gives the "RETRY + MEGA" bonus without watching a video. It does not unlock levels or change the rules. It is sold through Google Play Billing:

  • the purchase, your payment method and your purchase record are handled by Google in your Google account, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service;
  • we never see your payment details, your name or your email address, and we do not receive your purchase record on any server of ours;
  • the App asks Google Play only whether your Google account owns Premium (when the App starts and when you tap "Restore purchases") and stores the yes/no answer on your device (section 2).

Android versions from other stores (Samsung Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps) do not offer Premium.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share or sell any information with any third party, because we do not collect any. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects certain data and shares it with Google, as described in section 8. In the web versions, the game portal on which you play runs its own ads and data collection under its own privacy policy, as described in section 9.

5. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG). Purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval for purchases (Google Play parental controls / Family Link). Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

6. Security

No personal data is transmitted to or stored by us. Game progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's operating system. Purchases use Google Play's secure billing system; according to Google, data collected by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

7. Your rights and changes to this policy

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's personal data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; the locally stored game progress is fully under your control as described in section 2, your purchase record is managed by Google in your Google account, and requests about data processed by the ads SDK should be made to Google (section 8).

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that version is released.

8. Ads on Android (Google AdMob)

The Android App (from Google Play and from other Android stores) shows ads via Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. It includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. The iOS version contains no ads and no third-party SDKs.

When ads appear. Ads are never shown during a round or when the App opens. A full-screen ad may appear only when you leave the results screen after a won round, only after your first six wins, at most once every three minutes and at most once every three rounds. A rewarded video ("▶ RETRY + MEGA") is shown only when you tap it after a lost round. There are no banner ads. With Premium, no full-screen ads are shown and the rewarded bonus is given without a video.

What data is processed. We (Thanh Binh Games) do not receive, store, share or sell any of this data. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects the following data and shares it with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate the approximate location of your device);
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad/video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

According to Google, this data is encrypted in transit. To load ads, the App requests the internet and network-state permissions and the advertising ID permission.

Consent. Each time the App starts, it uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether a consent or privacy message is required in your region (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland under the GDPR, and in US states with applicable privacy laws). Ads are only requested after this check allows it, and personalised ads are only shown where you have consented and where permitted by law. Where required, Settings → "PRIVACY OPTIONS" lets you review or change your choices at any time. If your choice does not allow ads, the game still works fully; only the optional rewarded bonus is unavailable.

Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). Buying Premium removes the full-screen ads. Uninstalling the App stops all data processing by the App and the ads SDK.

How Google uses data. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Requests about data held by Google should be made to Google using the tools described there.

Game progress. As described in section 2, your current level, best score, total wins and settings stay only on your device and are never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else.

9. Web versions played in a browser (game portals)

Neon Drop Wars is also published as a web game that you play in a browser on game websites and platforms ("portals"), such as CrazyGames, Poki, GameDistribution, GameMonetize, Y8, Facebook Instant Games and itch.io. The web version itself does not collect, send or sell any personal data, and we (Thanh Binh Games) do not receive any data from it. It does not ask for an account, does not set cookies, contains no analytics or tracking code of our own, and does not send your progress to any server.

Information stored in your browser. To remember your progress, the web version saves the same game progress and settings as listed in section 2 (current level, best score, total wins, selected team colour, and sound/haptics settings) in your browser's local storage (localStorage) for the website that serves the game, under keys starting with neondropwars.: level, bestScore, wins, team, soundOn, hapticsOn. This information stays in your browser; the game does not send it to us or to anyone else. You can delete it by clearing the site data for that website in your browser settings. If your browser blocks local storage (for example in some private-browsing modes), progress is kept only in memory and is lost when you close the game.

Ads and data collection by the portal. On most portals the game is shown inside the portal's website or app, and the portal loads its own software development kit (SDK) into the game page from its own servers. The game uses this SDK only to tell the portal when loading has finished, when gameplay starts and stops, and when an ad may be shown; it does not pass your game progress or any personal data to it. The portal (not us) operates the ads and decides which ads are shown, and the portal and its advertising partners may use cookies, device or advertising identifiers, your IP address and similar technologies, and may ask for your consent where required by law. This processing is the responsibility of the portal under its own privacy policy, and any consent or privacy choices are offered and managed by the portal. The itch.io version contains no ads SDK and shows no ads.

Facebook Instant Games. When you play on Facebook or Messenger, Meta provides the platform and the ads. The game does not request your Facebook name, profile picture, friends list or player ID; Meta processes data about your use of its services under Meta's Privacy Policy.

Portal privacy policies. Please read the privacy policy of the portal on which you play, for example:

  • CrazyGames: crazygames.com/privacy-policy;
  • Poki: poki.com/en/privacy;
  • GameDistribution (Azerion): azerion.com/privacy-policy;
  • GameMonetize: gamemonetize.com/privacypolicy;
  • Y8: y8.com/privacy;
  • Facebook Instant Games (Meta): facebook.com/privacy/policy;
  • itch.io: itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy.

If you play the game on another website, that website's privacy policy applies to any data it collects.

Ads and children. Interstitial ads are never shown while a round is being played: they may appear before your first game (on portals that require it) or between rounds, at most about once every three minutes, and rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. Age requirements, ad content and children's privacy protections on each portal are set by that portal.

Analytics (Android, version 1.2.2 and later)

The Android App uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase SDK by Google) to see, in aggregate, how the game is played (for example which rounds are too hard) so we can improve it. It is not used to build advertising profiles, and the App has no crash-reporting SDK (no Crashlytics).

  • Events: gameplay events we define (for example a round started, completed or failed with its round number, mode, stars, moves or time; a hint or helper used and whether it was free, from a rewarded video or from Premium; a rewarded video offered or earned; an in-between ad shown; the Premium screen opened, a purchase started or completed) and events Google collects automatically (first open, app update, session start, engagement time). Events never contain your name, email, contacts, account or any text you type.
  • Identifiers and device information: a random Firebase app instance ID created for this installation (a new one is created if you reinstall the App or clear its data), device model, operating system and app version, language, screen size. Google derives an approximate location (country, region, city) from the IP address. Analytics does not collect the advertising ID (we switched that collection off; the AdMob SDK described above handles its own data).
  • Consent: Analytics starts with every Google Consent Mode v2 signal set to "denied". When Google's consent form (UMP) has resolved, the App passes your choice on: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland analytics storage is granted only if you agreed to storing information on your device and to measurement, and the ad-related signals follow your ad choices; elsewhere it is granted. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Who receives it, for how long: the data is sent encrypted to Google, which processes it for us as a service provider under the Google Analytics terms; we only see aggregated reports in the Firebase console and do not sell it. Event-level data is kept for 2 months (the Google Analytics data-retention setting) and then deleted; aggregated reports without identifiers may be kept longer. More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.
