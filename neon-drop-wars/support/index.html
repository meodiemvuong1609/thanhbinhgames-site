FAQ

How do I play? Pick a team, then tap above the board to drop balls. Score more points than the three rival teams before the 60-second timer ends to win and level up.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline. On Android only the ads and the Premium purchase use the internet. On Android, anonymous gameplay analytics are sent when a connection is available.

Are there ads or purchases? iOS: no ads and no in-app purchases. Android (from 1.2.0): a full-screen ad may appear when you leave the results screen after a won round (not in your first 6 wins, at most every 3 minutes and every 3 rounds, never during a round, never after a loss; no banners). After a loss, “▶ RETRY + MEGA” plays a short video only if you tap it and restarts the level with a mega ball (up to 3 times per level). On Google Play, “Premium — no ads, forever” is an optional one-time purchase that removes those ads and gives RETRY + MEGA without a video; it unlocks no levels. Find it, Restore purchases and Privacy options (when shown) in Settings.

How do I reset my progress? Uninstall and reinstall the app (Android: or clear the app's storage in Settings).

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We (Thanh Binh Games) run no servers and collect no personal data ourselves; on Android, Google AdMob and Google Play collect some data as described there.

Gameplay analytics (Android): from version 1.2.2 the app sends anonymous gameplay events (rounds won/lost, the mega retry, ads, the Premium screen) to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see which parts are too hard and improve them. No name, email or advertising ID; it follows your choice in Settings → Privacy options. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
