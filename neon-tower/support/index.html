FAQ

How do I play? Your tower in the middle fires on its own at the nearest shape in range. Stopping shapes and starting new waves gives energy ⬢. Tap an upgrade in the ATTACK, DEFENSE or UTILITY tab at any time to spend it. When the tower falls, the run ends and pays shards ◆.

Why can’t I buy that upgrade? A short line appears where you tapped and tells you why: not enough energy (with the amount still needed), the upgrade is at its maximum, or the tower is down.

What are shards and the Forge? Shards are kept forever. Spend them in the FORGE on 10 permanent upgrades (Core Power, Core Hull, Core Rate, Start Energy, Energy Gain, Auto Repair, Base Plating, Shard Yield, Head Start, Long Barrel); every new run starts with them.

How do tiers open? Reach wave 50 on a tier to open the next one. Higher tiers have tougher shapes and pay more shards. A locked tier tells you the wave it needs and your best so far.

What is the Daily run? Every day everyone gets the same run, based on your device’s local date, without Forge upgrades. Play it as often as you like; your best wave of the day is kept, and finishing a Daily run on consecutive days grows your streak. There are no ads, no revive and no ×2 in the Daily.

Is anything random? Where and which shapes arrive comes from a fixed seed per run; the combat itself has no chance: there are no random critical hits, and the same choices always give the same run.

What happens if I close the app during a run? The run is saved (its starting number and the upgrades you bought) and the menu offers CONTINUE RUN. Purchases made in the last moments before the app was closed may be undone.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline. Ads and the Premium purchase need a connection.

Are there ads or purchases? The free game may show a full-screen ad after you tap CONTINUE at the end of a run (never during a run, never in the Daily, not in your first runs, at most every 3 minutes). A video plays only when you tap “▶ Watch ad” to revive once or to double a run’s shards. PREMIUM (one-time purchase) removes the ads and gives those perks without a video. Use RESTORE PURCHASES in Settings on a new device.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage resets it.
