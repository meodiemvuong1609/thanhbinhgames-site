Sign & Merge PDF — Privacy policy

No ads · No account · Offline

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Publisher: Thanh Binh Games (HUONG MOC THANH DEVELOPMENT COMPANY LIMITED).

Summary

Sign & Merge PDF does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. The published app does not have the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

Data stored on your phone

Copies of the PDF files you open, the files the app creates (merged, signed, edited) and your saved signatures. This data is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions.

Other phone features

  • Files: the app opens only the PDF files you pick in the system file picker. It does not ask for storage access and cannot see your other files.
  • Sharing: when you tap Share or Open, the file goes to the app you choose, and that app's own policy applies.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything it stored. Android's own backup may keep a copy of app data if you have turned it on in your Google account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
