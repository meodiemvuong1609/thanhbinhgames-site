FAQ

How do I play? Tap the glowing core to make Energy. Spend Energy on shapes in the SHAPES tab: they orbit the core and make Energy by themselves. The buy button shows the price; the small button at the top of the list switches between buying 1, 10 or as many as you can afford.

Why can't I buy something? A short line appears right where you tapped and says why, for example how much Energy is missing, which shape unlocks an upgrade, or that a Lattice upgrade is already at its maximum.

What are milestones? Every shape doubles its production at 10, 25, 50 and 100 owned, then at every further 100. Its ring around the core gets more shapes and grows brighter.

What is a Collapse? Once you have earned 100B Energy in the current run, you can Collapse: your shapes, Energy and upgrades reset and you receive Shards. Every Shard you have ever earned adds +5% production forever, and Shards buy permanent Lattice upgrades (a brighter core, Auto-Pulse, Autobuild, Head Start).

Does the game run while the app is closed? Yes: when you come back, your structure has made Energy for the time you were away, up to 8 hours, calculated from your phone's clock in whole seconds. Auto-Pulse and Autobuild only work while the app is open. If you set the clock back, no offline Energy is given for that gap.

What is the daily gift? Once per day you can claim 15 minutes of your current production (at least 100 Energy). It is optional: skipping days loses nothing.

Are there ads? A short video plays only when you tap "▶ Watch ad" for a 2× production boost (4 hours) or to double your offline Energy, up to 3 rewards a day. From your third Collapse, a full-screen ad may appear after you tap CONTINUE on the Collapse screen, at most once every 3 minutes. No banners and no ads while you play. Premium (one-time purchase) removes these ads and gives the rewards without a video.

I bought Premium on another phone. Sign in with the same Google account and tap Settings → Restore purchases.

Does the game need internet? No. Only the optional ads and the Premium purchase use the internet.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage resets it.
