FAQ

How do I play? Drag one of the three pieces onto the board. When you let go it turns into glowing sand that pours down and settles. When grains of one colour connect from the left wall to the right wall, that ribbon clears and the sand above pours down.

What is the white prism light? Prism grains count as every colour, so they can bridge two halves of a ribbon. They clear together with every ribbon they touch.

What is FLOW? If a ribbon clears while the sand from your last drop is still moving, the chain grows (up to ×8) and every clear scores more. Dropping the next piece while the sand is flowing keeps the chain alive.

How are stars awarded? Win a level using at most two thirds of its piece budget for 3 stars, at most five sixths for 2 stars, otherwise 1 star.

What is the red laser line? Boss levels (every 10th level) have a laser line. The settled sand must stay below it, or the level is lost.

How do I get more helpers? REFRESH: +1 the first time you get 3 stars on a level. ROTATE: +1 the first time you clear each boss level, and +1 for your first Daily Sift of the day (max 9 each). Helpers are disabled in the Daily Sift. On Android, when ROTATE or REFRESH is used up on a normal level you can tap “▶ WATCH AD” for +1 (up to 3 per level); Premium gives it without a video. iOS has no ads and nothing to buy.

How does the Daily Sift work? Every day everyone gets the same start board and the same 30 pieces, based on the device's date. Play as often as you like; your best score of the day is kept. Playing on consecutive days grows your streak.

How do I unlock themes? Collect stars: Sunset at 30, Aurora at 90, Ember at 180 and Prisma at 300 stars.

I'm colour-blind. Every colour also has its own grain pattern (stripes, dots, lines, checks). Patterns are on by default and can be toggled in Settings.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline. On Android only the ads and the Premium purchase use the internet.

Are there ads or purchases? iOS: no ads and no in-app purchases. Android: the free game may show a full-screen ad after you tap NEXT on a win (not in your first 6 wins, at most every 3 minutes and every 3 levels, never during a level, never in the Daily Sift; no banners); a video plays only when you tap “▶ WATCH AD”. “Premium — no ads, forever” is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play that removes those ads and gives the +1 helper without a video; it unlocks no levels. Use Settings → Restore purchases on a new device; ad consent can be changed in Settings → Privacy options (when shown).

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app (or, on Android, clearing its storage) resets it.

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We (Thanh Binh Games) run no servers and collect no personal data ourselves; on Android, Google AdMob and Google Play collect some data as described there.
