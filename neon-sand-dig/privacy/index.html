Neon Sand Dig – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Neon Sand Dig (the "App"), published by Thanh Binh Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Google Android (and on Apple iOS if an iOS version is released).

Summary

Neon Sand Dig does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The App works completely offline, has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information from you. Specifically, the App:

  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or other device data;
  • does not use advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs;
  • does not connect to the internet (on Android it does not even request the network permission);
  • does not contain in-app purchases.

2. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few game settings locally on your device using the operating system's standard storage (SharedPreferences on Android):

  • highest unlocked level and the stars earned on each level;
  • Daily Dig streak, best streak, the date you last played, the streak freezes you hold (0 to 2) and your best saved sand of the day with its date;
  • whether the first tutorial has been shown;
  • sound, vibration and colour-pattern on/off settings;
  • whether the optional Daily reminder is on and its time, your total number of wins and the app version for which the rating card was last requested.

Levels you are playing are not saved; closing the App restarts the level. The Daily Dig is chosen from your device's local date; the date is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Optional Daily reminder (Android). The reminder is off by default. If you switch it on in Settings, the App asks for the system notification permission (Android 13 and later) and schedules a local notification on your device, up to 7 days ahead, at the time you choose. The App also uses the "receive boot completed" permission only to schedule it again after your device restarts, the App is updated or the time or time zone changes. The notification is created on your device; nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the App or in your device's notification settings.

Google Play rating card (Android). After you have won a few times, the App may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating card, at most once per app version; Google decides whether it appears. The card is Google's own service and is governed by Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The App itself sends no data to the card and does not receive your rating or comment; if you choose to submit one, Google processes it under its own policy.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your device's own backups (for example Google backup) according to your device settings, which are governed by Google's privacy policy. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage.

3. Sharing initiated by you

When you tap SHARE on a Daily Dig result, the App prepares a short text on your device (the game name, the date, the number of taps and hints, the sand you saved and one coloured emoji square per cup) and opens your device's standard share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app or contact in that sheet; whatever you then share is handled by the app you choose, not by us.

4. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not share or sell information with any third party.

5. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Since the App collects no personal information at all, no personal information from children is collected, used or disclosed. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

6. Security

No personal data is transmitted or stored by us. Game progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's operating system.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's personal data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; the locally stored game progress is fully under your control as described in section 2.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled (for example by adding online features), we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that version is released.
