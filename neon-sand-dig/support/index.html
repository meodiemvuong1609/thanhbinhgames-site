FAQ

How do I play? Tap a glowing gate: it vanishes and the coloured sand above it pours down. Fill every cup past its dashed line with sand of its own colour. Tap a dirt block to break it into mud.

Why did my cup turn grey? A cup takes only a little wrong sand (one tenth of its size). More wrong sand or mud spoils it and the level is lost. A cup that is already full to the brim lets wrong sand slide off its top, so fill the cup first, then release the rest.

Do I have to open everything? Yes. A level is cleared when every gate and dirt block has been opened and every cup is full and clean. If every gate is open but a cup is below its line, or the sand left can no longer fill the cups, the level is lost.

What is the white sand? White prism sand counts as the right colour for every cup.

How do hints and stars work? HINT glows a good next tap; you get 3 hints on each new level. Clear a level without a hint for 3 stars, with one hint for 2 stars, otherwise 1 star. RESTART is free and unlimited.

Why can't I tap right now? Taps work only when the sand has settled; one tap made while sand is moving is kept and done when it settles. When a tap or button can't be used, a short line on screen tells you why.

How does the Daily Dig work? Every day there is a new cavern, chosen from your device's date, the same for everyone on the same date. Play it as often as you like; your best saved sand of the day is kept. Playing on consecutive days grows your streak, and every 7 days of streak earns a streak freeze (❄, up to 2) that covers a missed day automatically.

How do I get the Daily reminder? It is off by default. Turn it on in Settings, allow notifications and pick a time. It is a local notification from your phone and is skipped on days you already played.

I'm colour-blind. Every colour also has its own glyph on the cup and its own grain pattern. Patterns are on by default and can be toggled in Settings (COLOUR PATTERNS).

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Are there ads or purchases? No ads and no in-app purchases.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage resets it. A level you are in the middle of is not saved.
