Questions

How do I play? Each level is a neon picture cut into square pieces. One piece already glows in the empty frame. A piece fits once a piece next to it (up, down, left or right) is in the frame. Tap the top piece of a column, or the piece you drew from the stock: if it fits, it flies into its place. Place every piece to finish the picture.

A piece does not fit yet. What now? Tap it, then tap another column: you can park it on a top piece that touches it in the picture, or in an empty column. A column holds at most 8 pieces. Face-down pieces turn over when they are uncovered. Tap the stock to draw a piece; when the stock is empty, tapping it turns the drawn pile over again.

Why can't I move there? When a move is not allowed, a short line appears where you tapped and tells you why (for example: not connected yet, the pieces do not touch in the picture, or the column is full) and what to do instead.

Can every level be finished? Yes. Every deal is checked by a solver before you see it. UNDO is free and unlimited, so a wrong turn is never final.

How are stars awarded? 3 stars when your number of moves is at most the level's par plus a quarter, 2 stars when it is at most twice the par (at least par + 3), otherwise 1 star. Using a hint caps the level at 2 stars.

Hints and ads. Every level and the Daily give 2 free hints. In levels, once they are used, HINT reads "▶ Watch ad": a short video gives one more hint (up to 3 per level). Premium gives those hints without a video. The Daily never shows ads.

How does the Daily work? Every day everyone gets the same deal and picture, based on your device's local date. Finishing it keeps your streak going; every 7 days in a row earns a streak freeze (❄, up to 2) that covers one missed day.

Where are my pictures? Every finished picture is kept in the ALBUM on the menu.

Premium and restoring a purchase. Premium is a one-time purchase that removes ads for good. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account.

Ad privacy choices. Settings → Privacy options reopens the consent form when it applies in your region.

Lost progress? Progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it, unless your own Android backup restores it.

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We collect no personal data ourselves; ads are served by Google AdMob as described there.
