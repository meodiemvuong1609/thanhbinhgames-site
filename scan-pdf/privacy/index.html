Scan PDF: Document Scanner — Privacy policy

No ads · No account · Offline

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Publisher: Thanh Binh Games (HUONG MOC THANH DEVELOPMENT COMPANY LIMITED).

Summary

Scan PDF: Document Scanner does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no account. The published app does not have the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

Data stored on your phone

The PDF files you scan, a preview image of each first page and the document names. This data is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it.

Permissions

  • Network state (view only) — added by Google Play services' scanner library. It only lets the phone check whether a network exists; it does not let the app use the internet.

Other phone features

  • Scanning: the app opens the document scanner of Google Play services, which uses the camera on the app's behalf and returns the pages to the app. The app itself does not ask for camera access. Google's handling of that service is described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Sharing: when you tap Share or Open, the file goes to the app you choose, and that app's own policy applies.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything it stored. Android's own backup may keep a copy of app data if you have turned it on in your Google account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
