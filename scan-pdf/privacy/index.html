Scan PDF: Document Scanner — Privacy policy

Free with ads · No account

Last updated: 27 November 2026. Publisher: Thanh Binh Games (HUONG MOC THANH DEVELOPMENT COMPANY LIMITED).

Summary

We (Thanh Binh Games) run no servers and do not receive, sell or share the content you create in Scan PDF: Document Scanner. There is no account. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. When your phone is online, Google's ad SDK collects some device data, described below; that data goes to Google, not to us.

Data stored on your phone

The PDF files you scan, a preview image of each first page and the document names. This data is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform run inside the app to show banner and full-screen ads. According to Google, they may collect: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions (views and taps), and diagnostic and performance data. Google uses this data to serve, measure and personalise ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time from the app's menu (“Ad privacy choices”). Read how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — used only to load ads from Google AdMob and to ask for ad consent. Your own files, texts and settings are not sent anywhere.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — read by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Other phone features

  • Scanning: the app opens the document scanner of Google Play services, which uses the camera on the app's behalf and returns the pages to the app. The app itself does not ask for camera access. Google's handling of that service is described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Sharing: when you tap Share or Open, the file goes to the app you choose, and that app's own policy applies.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything it stored. Android's own backup may keep a copy of app data if you have turned it on in your Google account. Data collected by Google for ads is managed by Google; see the link above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
