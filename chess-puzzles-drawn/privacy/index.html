Chess Puzzles, Drawn – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 (version 1.1.0)

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Chess Puzzles, Drawn (the "App"), published by Thanh Binh Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Google Android.

Summary: We (Thanh Binh Games) do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data, and we run no server for the App. The App has no accounts and no analytics of our own. From version 1.1.0 the free version shows ads through Google AdMob; the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects certain data and shares it with Google to show ads (section 5). The only paid item, the optional "Full pack", is bought through Google Play, and Google handles the payment; it also removes the ads.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information from you. Specifically, the App:

  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or other device data;
  • contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code of our own (the Google Mobile Ads SDK is described in section 5);
  • does not send any information to us; the puzzles work offline.

2. In-app purchase (Google Play)

Puzzles 1–30 and the Daily puzzle are free. The optional one-time "Full pack" unlocks puzzles 31–110 and removes the ads (the extra hints then come at once, without a video). It is sold through Google Play Billing:

  • the purchase, your payment method and your purchase record are handled by Google in your Google account, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service;
  • we never see your payment details, your name or your email address, and we do not receive your purchase record on any server of ours;
  • the App asks Google Play only whether your Google account owns the Full pack (when the App starts or returns to the screen, and when you tap "Restore purchases") and stores a single "unlocked" yes/no value on your device. To make this work, the Google Play Billing library included in the App uses the network permissions (internet and network state) on Android; the App's own code makes no network requests of its own;
  • refunds are handled by Google Play. After a refund, the App locks the Full pack again the next time Google Play reports that your account no longer owns it.

3. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few game settings locally on your device using Android's standard storage (SharedPreferences):

  • the stars earned on each puzzle and the puzzle you are on;
  • the pieces you placed in the open puzzle and in today's Daily puzzle, and the hints used there;
  • Daily puzzle streak, best streak, the date you last solved a Daily puzzle and the streak freezes you hold (0 to 2);
  • sound, haptics, reduce-motion and show-attacked-squares settings;
  • whether the Full pack is unlocked (a cached yes/no, see section 2);
  • the number of puzzles you have solved since installing (so that no ad appears in your first puzzles) and the extra hints granted in the open puzzle.

The Daily puzzle is chosen from your device's local date; the date is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your device's own backups or device-to-device transfer (Google backup) according to your device settings, which are governed by Google's privacy policy. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage.

4. Actions you start

  • Share. When you tap Share on a solved Daily puzzle, the App prepares a short text on your device (the App name, the date, the task and the board drawn with emoji squares and chess symbols) and opens your device's standard share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app or contact in that sheet; whatever you then share is handled by the app you choose, not by us.
  • Watch the videos. The "Watch the videos ↗" button in the menu opens our TikTok page (https://www.tiktok.com/@numberpuzzlesdrawn) in your browser. The App sends no information with it; TikTok's own privacy policy applies to what you do there.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob, a service provided by Google, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

When ads appear. A full-screen ad may appear only after you tap "Next ▶" on a solved puzzle: never while you solve a puzzle, never in the Daily puzzle, never before you have solved 6 puzzles, and at most about once every three minutes. When the 3 free hints of a puzzle are used, an optional "▶ Watch ad" button lets you watch a rewarded ad for one more hint (up to 3 more per puzzle, not in the Daily). There are no banner ads and no ads when the App opens. With the Full pack there are no ads.

What data is processed. We do not receive any of it. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects the following data and shares it with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: IP address (which may be used to estimate your approximate location); product interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views; diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage; and device identifiers (the Android advertising ID and app set ID). According to Google, this data is encrypted in transit. To load ads, the App requests the internet, network-state and advertising ID permissions.

Consent. Each time the App starts, it uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether a consent or privacy message is required in your region (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in US states with applicable privacy laws). Ads are only requested after this check allows it, and personalised ads are only shown where you have consented and where permitted by law. Where required, Settings → Privacy options lets you review or change your choices at any time.

Your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Android settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying the Full pack removes the ads. Uninstalling the App stops all data processing by the App and the ads SDK. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5a. Sharing of information

We do not collect any information, so we do not share or sell information with any third party. The data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is shared with Google as described in section 5.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children; ad content is limited to material suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we collect no personal information at all. Purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval for purchases (Google Play parental controls / Family Link). Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

7. Security

No personal data is transmitted to or stored by us. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google. Game progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's operating system. Purchases use Google Play's secure billing system.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's personal data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; the locally stored game progress is fully under your control as described in section 3, your purchase record is managed by Google in your Google account, and data collected for ads is handled by Google (see section 5 for your choices).

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled (for example by adding online features), we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that version is released.
