Chess Puzzles, Drawn – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Chess Puzzles, Drawn (the "App"), published by Thanh Binh Games ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Google Android.

Summary: Chess Puzzles, Drawn does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The App has no accounts, no ads and no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we run no server for it. The only paid item, the optional "Full pack", is bought through Google Play, and Google handles the payment.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information from you. Specifically, the App:

  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or other device data;
  • does not use advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs;
  • does not send any information to us; the puzzles work offline.

2. In-app purchase (Google Play)

Puzzles 1–30 and the Daily puzzle are free. The optional one-time "Full pack" unlocks puzzles 31–110. It is sold through Google Play Billing:

  • the purchase, your payment method and your purchase record are handled by Google in your Google account, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service;
  • we never see your payment details, your name or your email address, and we do not receive your purchase record on any server of ours;
  • the App asks Google Play only whether your Google account owns the Full pack (when the App starts or returns to the screen, and when you tap "Restore purchases") and stores a single "unlocked" yes/no value on your device. To make this work, the Google Play Billing library included in the App uses the network permissions (internet and network state) on Android; the App's own code makes no network requests;
  • refunds are handled by Google Play. After a refund, the App locks the Full pack again the next time Google Play reports that your account no longer owns it.

3. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few game settings locally on your device using Android's standard storage (SharedPreferences):

  • the stars earned on each puzzle and the puzzle you are on;
  • the pieces you placed in the open puzzle and in today's Daily puzzle, and the hints used there;
  • Daily puzzle streak, best streak, the date you last solved a Daily puzzle and the streak freezes you hold (0 to 2);
  • sound, haptics, reduce-motion and show-attacked-squares settings;
  • whether the Full pack is unlocked (a cached yes/no, see section 2).

The Daily puzzle is chosen from your device's local date; the date is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your device's own backups or device-to-device transfer (Google backup) according to your device settings, which are governed by Google's privacy policy. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage.

4. Actions you start

  • Share. When you tap Share on a solved Daily puzzle, the App prepares a short text on your device (the App name, the date, the task and the board drawn with emoji squares and chess symbols) and opens your device's standard share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app or contact in that sheet; whatever you then share is handled by the app you choose, not by us.
  • Watch the videos. The "Watch the videos ↗" button in the menu opens our TikTok page (https://www.tiktok.com/@numberpuzzlesdrawn) in your browser. The App sends no information with it; TikTok's own privacy policy applies to what you do there.

5. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not share or sell information with any third party.

6. Children's privacy

The App is rated for everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Since the App collects no personal information at all, no personal information from children is collected, used or disclosed. Purchases go through Google Play, where parents can require approval for purchases (Google Play parental controls / Family Link). Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

7. Security

No personal data is transmitted to or stored by us. Game progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's operating system. Purchases use Google Play's secure billing system.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's personal data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; the locally stored game progress is fully under your control as described in section 3, and your purchase record is managed by Google in your Google account.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled (for example by adding online features), we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that version is released.
