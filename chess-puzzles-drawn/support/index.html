FAQ

How do I play? The task is written above the board. Tap an empty square to place a piece, tap a piece to lift it. The squares a piece attacks light up as you place it.

What are Peace and Guard puzzles? Peace: place the given number of pieces so that no piece attacks another. Guard: place exactly the given number of pieces so that every square holds a piece or is attacked by one.

Why does a piece "see through" another? As in the classic N-queens and domination problems, attacks are counted on the empty board: pieces do not block each other.

What are the ✕ squares and the circled pieces? ✕ squares can't hold a piece (and in Guard puzzles they need no guard). Circled pieces are fixed: they are part of the puzzle and can't be lifted.

How do hints and stars work? You have 3 hints per puzzle. A hint shows one square of a solution that fits the pieces you placed, or marks the one piece to lift first. 3 stars with no hint, 2 with one hint, 1 otherwise. Restart is always free.

How does the Daily puzzle work? A new puzzle every day, based on your device's date, always free. Solving it on consecutive days grows your streak. Every 7 days in a row earns a streak freeze (❄, up to 2) that covers a missed day. After solving it you can share the board as an emoji card.

What is free and what is paid? Puzzles 1–30 and the Daily puzzle are free. The optional Full pack is a one-time purchase through Google Play that unlocks puzzles 31–110. It is not a subscription, and there are no ads.

I bought the Full pack but puzzles are still locked. Make sure the Play Store is signed in with the Google account that bought it, connect to the internet, then open Menu → "Restore purchases". If the payment is still pending (some payment methods take time), the puzzles unlock when Google Play confirms it.

Refunds? Purchases are processed by Google Play. Request a refund through Google Play (your Order history), or email us and we will help.

Does the game need internet? No. Only buying or restoring the Full pack needs a connection to Google Play.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage resets it; the Full pack comes back with "Restore purchases".

Accessibility: TalkBack reads every square (name, piece, "attacked by 2" or "unguarded"). Settings has sound, haptics, reduce motion and show attacked squares.

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We collect no personal data; purchases are handled by Google Play.
