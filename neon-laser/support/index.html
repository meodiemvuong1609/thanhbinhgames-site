FAQ

How do I play? Tap a mirror, splitter or prism to turn it a quarter turn. Light changes instantly. Send every laser into the ring of its colour; the level is clear when every ring receives exactly its colour and no beam touches a crystal.

How do colours mix? Light adds up: red + blue = magenta, red + green = yellow, green + blue = cyan, all three = white. A ring that receives several beams gets their mix, and it must match exactly. The dots under a ring show the colours it needs (red left, green middle, blue right).

What does a splitter do? From level 16, dashed glass pieces let light pass and also reflect it, so one beam becomes two; two beams meeting in a splitter mix on both outputs.

What does a prism do? From level 26, a prism splits light into its parts: red leaves to the left of its arrow, green straight along it, blue to its right.

What are crystals? From level 36, keep every beam away from the violet crystals. A lit crystal turns red and blocks the win until it is dark again.

Why can't I turn that? Only mirrors, splitters and prisms turn. When you tap anything else, a short line appears where you tapped and tells you why.

How are stars awarded? Every level's par is the fewest taps that solve it, proven by a solver. 3 stars at par or better, 2 stars within a small margin (at least 2 more taps), otherwise 1 star. Using a HINT caps the level at 2 stars. RESTART is free and resets the tap count.

How do I get more hints? You start with 3. Each first clear of every 5th level and your first Daily of the day give +1 (at most 9). With no hint left you can watch a short ad for one more (up to 3 per level), or buy Premium to get them without a video.

How does the Daily work? Every day everyone gets the same board, based on your device's local date. Play as often as you like; your best number of taps of the day is kept. Hints are off in the Daily. Playing on consecutive days grows your streak, and every 7-day streak earns a streak freeze (❄, hold up to 2) that covers a missed day.

How does the Daily reminder work? It is off by default. Turn it on in Settings, allow notifications when Android asks (Android 13 and later) and pick a time. It is a local notification scheduled on your phone; it is not sent on a day you already finished the Daily.

I’m colour-blind. Lasers and rings show red-green-blue dots at fixed positions (red left, green middle, blue right), so you can read every colour without seeing the hue. The dots are on by default and can be switched off in Settings. TalkBack reads every cell, including the colour it needs and the light it gets.

Are there ads or purchases? The free game shows a full-screen ad now and then after you tap NEXT on a win (never during play, never in the Daily) and a short video only when you tap “▶ Watch ad” for an extra hint. Premium (one-time purchase) removes the ads for good. Use Settings → Restore purchases on a new device.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline; only ads and purchases use the network.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage resets it. A level in progress is not saved: closing the app restarts that level.
